# Buttondown brand guidelines

## Our name
Our name is Buttondown with one capital B. Not ButtonDown, Button Down, Buttondown Email, or –&nbsp;heaven help us –&nbsp;ButtDown. (It’s happened.)

## Our logo
- Don’t alter or edit our logo or other imagery in any way without [asking us](mailto:support@buttondown.com) first.
- Don’t change the color of our logo.
- Don’t put our logo or assets in any context that implies a business relationship or affiliation.
- Use only the white or black backgrounds when embedding our logo.

## Our colors
- Buttondown Blue (`#0069FF`)
- Eigengray (`#3D3E4C`)
- Ice White (`#F9FAFB`)

## Questions?
Just [send us a message](mailto:support@buttondown.com), and we'd be happy to help! 💙
